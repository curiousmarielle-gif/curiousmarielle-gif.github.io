Section 2 of 10. What alpha-gal syndrome, or A-G-S, is.

*PAUSE 0.75 SECONDS — DO NOT READ*

Alpha-gal syndrome is an acquired, immunoglobulin E–mediated, or I-G-E–mediated, allergy to galactose-alpha-1,3-galactose, a carbohydrate in nonprimate mammalian tissue and products. Tick bites can induce alpha-gal I-G-E. Diagnosis requires compatible symptoms plus alpha-gal-specific I-G-E; sensitization alone is not A-G-S.
